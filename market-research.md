### Pattern Analysis

The research shows that JavaScript/TypeScript and React are the most
frequently mentioned technologies in the five listings. Node.js,
database technologies, and APIs also appear regularly. Angular,
Python, and Docker appear in fewer listings, while technologies such
as Next.js, Kubernetes, Java, and C#/.NET appear only once.
